Since 1 April 2026, TDS/TCS moved from the Income-tax Act, 1961 (old sections like 194C, 194J, 206C) to the Income-tax Act, 2025. Non-salary resident TDS now sits under Section 393, salary TDS under Section 392, and TCS under Section 394 — each payment type gets a 4-digit code used in returns/challans instead of the old section name. Rates and thresholds are mostly unchanged; this is a renumbering, not a rewrite. Both old and new references are shown for each entry. A handful of codes are still settling with CBDT — verify against the live TRACES/e-filing dropdown before filing.
